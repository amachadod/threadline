Northwind SaaS is remote-first. You can work from anywhere in the EU.

- Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 CET. Outside them, nobody expects a reply.
- Home office budget: €500 per year for a desk, chair, screen or headset. Claim it through Expenses.
- Write things down: decisions go in Notion, tasks go in Asana, quick questions go in Slack.
