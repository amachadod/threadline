Welcome to Northwind SaaS. This page is your first stop.

## Your first week
- Day 1: set up your laptop, your password manager and two-factor authentication. See Security basics.
- Day 2: read Remote work, Expenses and the AI use policy.
- Day 3: join your team's channels in Slack and your team's project in Asana.
- By Friday: book a 25-minute intro call with each person on your team.

Questions about this page go to the Operations team in #ask-ops.
