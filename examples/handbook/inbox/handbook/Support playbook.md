# Support playbook

Owner: Support team
Last edited time: September 2, 2026 1:20 PM

## Response times
First response time: within 4 business hours for every ticket. Urgent tickets (the product is down) within 1 hour.

## Tone
Use the Brand voice. Thank the customer, say what you'll do and when.

## Escalation
If a fix needs engineering, create a task in Asana and tell the customer you've done it.
