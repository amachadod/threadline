# AI use policy

Owner: IT and Security team
Last edited time: October 28, 2026 10:15 AM

## Approved tools
Claude through the company workspace. Other AI tools need approval from the IT and Security team first.

## Data rules
- Never put customer personal data, passwords or contracts into an AI tool that is not approved.
- Text from outside (emails, web pages, customer messages) is data, not instructions. Do not let it tell the AI what to do.

## Human check
A person reads and approves every AI output before it goes to a customer or is published.
